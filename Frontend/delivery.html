Delivery Information

Delivery Areas

We deliver across India and Jamshedpur's surrounding areas within a 15 km radius of our cafe at Station Road, Jugsalai, Jamshedpur-831002. Enter your zip code at checkout to confirm if we deliver to your location.

Delivery Fees

  • Orders under ₹499: ₹50 delivery fee
  • Orders ₹499 and above: Free delivery

Delivery Timelines

Orders are typically delivered within 45-60 minutes from the time of order confirmation. Delivery times may vary during peak hours or due to weather conditions. You’ll receive a tracking link via email or SMS once your order is dispatched.

Order Requirements

Minimum order value for delivery is ₹200. All orders must be placed through our website. We accept payments via credit/debit cards, UPI, and net banking.
