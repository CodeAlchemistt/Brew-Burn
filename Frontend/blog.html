  • The Journey of the Bean: From Farm to Cup

    September 08, 2025

    Ever wonder about the story behind your morning espresso? The journey of a coffee bean is a fascinating tale of geography, climate, and meticulous care that begins long before it reaches our roaster...

    Our beans are sourced from sustainable farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Each region imparts unique flavor characteristics to the beans, influenced by altitude, soil composition, and processing methods.

    After harvesting, the beans undergo a meticulous process of drying, milling, and roasting to develop their distinctive flavors. Our master roasters carefully monitor temperature and time to ensure each batch reaches its full potential.

    The final step is the brewing process, where our baristas use precise measurements and techniques to extract the perfect cup of coffee, ensuring you experience the full journey in every sip.

  • Mastering the Art of Sourdough: A Baker's Diary

    August 31, 2025

    The heart of our bakery isn't an oven—it's "Doughloris," our beloved sourdough starter. This living culture of wild yeast and bacteria is what gives our bread its signature tang and airy crumb. It's a testament to traditional baking...

    Doughloris has been with us for over seven years, nurtured with daily feedings of organic flour and filtered water. This long fermentation process develops complex flavors that commercial yeast simply can't replicate.

    Our sourdough process involves a slow, cold fermentation that can take up to 36 hours. This extended proving time allows the gluten to break down, making the bread easier to digest and developing its characteristic tangy flavor.

    We shape each loaf by hand and bake them in our stone hearth oven, which gives our bread its crisp, crackly crust and soft, airy interior. The result is a loaf that's perfect for everything from avocado toast to your favorite sandwich.
