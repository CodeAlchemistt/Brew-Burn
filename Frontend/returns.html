Returns Policy

Our Commitment

At Brew & Burn, we strive to ensure your satisfaction with every order. If something isn’t right, we’re here to help with our hassle-free returns policy.

Return Conditions

  • Items must be returned within 24 hours of delivery due to the perishable nature of our products.
  • Items must be unconsumed, in their original packaging, and in the same condition as delivered.
  • Returns are accepted for incorrect, damaged, or defective items only.
  • Non-perishable items (e.g., packaged coffee beans) may be returned within 7 days if unopened.

Return Process

To initiate a return, please contact our support team at support@brew&burn.com or call +91-776-XXXX-618 with your order ID and reason for return. We’ll provide instructions for returning the item to our Jamshedpur location. Refunds or replacements will be processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned items.

Non-Returnable Items

Custom orders, gift cards, and partially consumed items are non-returnable. Delivery fees are non-refunded unless the return is due to our error.
